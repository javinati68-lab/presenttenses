Activity 5: Reading Comprehension

William's Typical Day

William is a high school student who leads a very busy life. Every morning, he wakes up at 6:30 AM to prepare for the day. He usually has a quick breakfast, which often consists of cereal and a glass of orange juice. After breakfast, he catches the bus to school at 7:15 AM. The ride takes about thirty minutes, so he uses this time to review his notes or listen to music.

School starts at 8:00 AM. William’s favorite subject is history because he loves learning about the past. However, he finds mathematics quite difficult. Fortunately, his teacher is very helpful and explains the concepts clearly. He has five classes before lunch, which is at 12:30 PM. He usually eats a sandwich and talks with his friends in the cafeteria.

In the afternoon, William has two more classes. When school finishes at 3:00 PM, he doesn't go straight home. He is part of the school soccer team, so he attends practice twice a week. On the other days, he goes to the library to finish his homework. He prefers doing his homework at school because it is quieter than his house.

William usually gets home around 5:30 PM. He helps his mother prepare dinner. They eat together as a family at 7:00 PM. After dinner, William likes to relax. He sometimes watches a television show or plays video games with his younger brother. By 9:30 PM, he is usually very tired. He packs his backpack for the next day, brushes his teeth, and goes to bed. He reads a book for a few minutes before falling asleep, ready to start all over again the next day.
